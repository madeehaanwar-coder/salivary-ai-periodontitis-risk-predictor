# Data

## Source study
Anwar, M., Alam, B.F., Ali, S., Tariq, S.F., Aali, K., Abrar, E., Alotaibi, D.H., Alsinaidi, A.A., Alrahlah, A., & Vohra, F. (2022). Evaluation of Salivary Mucin, Amylase, Protein Profile, and Periodontal Parameters among Hypertensive and Diabetic Patients. Applied Sciences, 12(15), 7407. https://doi.org/10.3390/app12157407

**Ethical approval:** Bahria University Ethical Review Committee (ERC 73/2020).**
Design:** cross-sectional, three age-matched groups of n=20 each — healthy controls, Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), and hypertension (HTN).

**Variables:** unstimulated salivary flow rate, salivary pH, mucin, amylase, and total protein concentration; periodontal parameters (PPD, CAL, BOP); PESA/PISA inflammatory burden indices; periodontitis severity; antihypertensive/hypoglycemic medication use.

## Synthetic dataset (used in this repo)
Generated to match the published group-level means and standard deviations (Tables 1, 2, and 4 of the article above) — a per-subject reconstruction consistent with the reported summary statistics, not a copy of any real participant's values. See `notebooks/01_synthetic_data_generation.ipynb`.
